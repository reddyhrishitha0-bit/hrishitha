About me

I'm an M.Pharm graduate in Pharmaceutical Quality Assurance with a six-month Regulatory Affairs internship at Hetero Labs Limited. I reviewed Product Dossier Reports, assisted with CTD/eCTD dossier preparation, supported SmPC and PIL review, and entered and tracked regulatory data in RIIMS. Academic research, not employment experience

Lifecycle Management of Generic Solid Oral Dosage Forms in the European Union: From Registration to Post-Approval Compliance

M.Pharm thesis, Manipal College of Pharmaceutical Sciences, MAHE

Project overview

The thesis examines how a generic solid oral dosage form is handled in the EU, from the first registration through to the changes and compliance duties that follow approval. The aim was to understand the regulatory framework and documentation that apply across a product's life.

Regulatory topics studied

  • EU marketing authorization framework
  • CTD documentation and Module 3
  • Post-approval variations
  • ICH Q12
  • Lifecycle management and continuing compliance

Relevance to Regulatory Affairs

The project built my academic understanding of regulatory requirements, dossier structure and how post-approval changes are documented and classified. It gives me a framework for understanding why documents must be accurate, current and consistent over time.

Sample Regulatory Documentation Tracker and Completeness Checklist

I built this mock tracker in Excel to practise the organisation a project assistant needs: following documents by version, owner, due date and review status, and noting what is still outstanding. All study IDs, document IDs, dates and names are fictional.

Document completeness checklist

Document titleCorrect versionDate presentRequired sections presentFile name verifiedReview statusOutstanding action

How I would maintain it

  • Update the row the same day a document changes status.
  • Raise the version number with every revision and never overwrite earlier entries.
  • Use a status drop-down (Not started, In review, Complete) so entries stay uniform.
  • In Excel, flag rows where the due date has passed and the completed date is empty, for example with =IF(AND(F2<TODAY(),G2=""),"Overdue","").
  • Apply conditional formatting to colour status cells.

What it shows

Organised document tracking, version control habits, a repeatable completeness check and attention to detail, all of which support a Regulatory Project Assistant. It does not show experience with CTIS, eTMF or any company system.
